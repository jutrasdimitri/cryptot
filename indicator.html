Most indicators shout “buy” and “sell” all day long. CryptoT Confluence does the opposite: it stays quiet until five independent technical readings actually agree — and you see that agreement in one number, the Confluence Score, X out of 5, on your chart at all times.

What it is

CryptoT Confluence is an original TradingView indicator built for learners who want to read a chart the way experienced analysts do: structure first, momentum second, confirmation before action. A full signal appears only when at least 4 of 5 components align with the trend — and, since v1.1, only inside the regime context the historical audit actually validated. The rest of the time, the score tells you the most useful thing a beginner can hear: not yet.

It was created by Dimitri “Dim” Jutras, the educator behind CryptoT — daily crypto education in English and French, and the Power 5 Starter Guide. The indicator simply encodes the same discipline taught on the channel: multiple confirmations, no leverage gambling, never quit learning.

The five components

C1 — Trend: price position vs. fast/slow EMAs. No signal ever fires against the dominant trend — it is also the hard filter: no buy alignment can trigger below the slow EMA.

C2 — Structure: the sequence of confirmed pivot highs and lows (higher highs / higher lows, or the mirror). It also draws the automatic support & resistance levels from those same pivots — calculated, never hand-drawn.

C3 — Momentum: RSI and the MACD histogram must agree before they vote. It confirms; it doesn’t predict.

C4 — Flow: a composite of public, published formulas (MFI, recentered RSI, recentered stochastic, −100 to +100) asking one question: is money actually behind this move?

C5 — Volume: a confirmation point awarded only when participation (relative volume, RVOL ≥ 1.2) is real. A move without volume doesn’t earn the point.

The regime layer — why context decides

The first version of this indicator scored alignment and nothing else. Our historical audit (full results below) showed that the same alignment means opposite things depending on where price sits versus its EMA 200 — so v1.1 qualifies every event by regime:

Full BUY signal (“CONF”) — buy alignment with price above the EMA 200: trend continuation territory, the context the audit validated on the daily timeframe.

Full SELL signal (“CONF”) — sell alignment breaking down while price is still above the EMA 200: an early breakdown, the second validated context.

“REBOND” — observation only. A buy alignment below the EMA 200 is a counter-trend bounce, not a buy signal. Historically its median 20-bar outcome was negative (see the audit) — the indicator labels it so you watch it, not chase it.

“CAPITULATION” — avoid. A full bearish alignment inside an established downtrend marked exhaustion more often than continuation in the audit — selling or shorting there meant standing in front of sharp rebounds. The indicator warns instead of signalling.

An anti-echo cooldown (10 bars per direction) also stops the same signal from re-firing bar after bar: in the raw version, about 70% of signals were echoes of the previous one; in v1.1 that share is cut to about 45%.

How to read the score

0–2 / 5 — Noise. The readings disagree or are mostly neutral. Do nothing. This is most of the time — and it’s the point.

3 / 5 — Partial alignment. Worth watching. Check the automatic support/resistance levels nearby.

4–5 / 5 — Strong alignment. The only zone where a full signal can appear, when the regime qualifies it — still your decision, with your own risk plan.

Recommended use: daily (1D) to decide the direction, 4H to watch the setup. Below 1H is not recommended — the indicator was designed for 4H/1D-style reading, not scalping.

The audit — what the numbers say

Before publishing this tool, we replayed its exact rules over historical data and measured what happened after each signal. This section publishes the results — including the parts that flatter nobody. That is the deal: published audit statistics, and the limits printed next to them.

Method. Audit run October 10, 2026, on public OKX data — daily candles from February 14, 2022 to October 9, 2026, and 4-hour candles from August 3, 2025 to October 10, 2026 — across BTC, ETH, ADA, SOL and XRP. 1,820 events logged; signals confirmed at candle close only (no repainting by construction); one frozen parameter set, nothing tuned after the fact. Outcomes measured over the following 5, 10 and 20 bars. No fees, slippage or position sizing: this audits signal timing and context, not a trading strategy.

The two contexts that held up — daily timeframe, full signals only:

Full signal (daily)NWhat the audit measured
BUY, price above EMA 200154 Median move after 10 bars: +1.15% · mean after 20 bars: +5.70% · 52% closed positive
SELL, early breakdown (still above EMA 200)86 The favourable move came first in 52.3% of the ±3% race · median move after 20 bars: +1.61% in the signal’s direction

The limits — published on purpose:

▸ Pooled across every context, the ±3% race is roughly a coin flip (full BUY: 44.7% favourable-first vs 45.6% adverse-first, random baseline 43.5% / 47.6%; full SELL: 47.3% vs 44.0%, baseline 47.6% / 43.5%). On the 4H timeframe, medians sit near zero. There is no general timing edge in these numbers — the measured value lives in the two daily contexts above, and in knowing which contexts to avoid.

▸ The avoided contexts, measured: “REBOND” labels (buy alignment below the EMA 200, N = 428) had a median 20-bar outcome of −1.35% — hence observation only. “CAPITULATION” labels on the daily timeframe (N = 272) were followed by a median adverse excursion of −11.69% — selling there meant standing in front of rebounds. Hence avoid.

▸ Excursions cut both ways: the median worst adverse excursion after a daily full BUY signal was about −9% over 20 bars. That measured fact is why the included guide teaches invalidation sized to the instrument — never a fixed −3% stop.

Past signals say nothing about future results. These statistics describe how this specific tool behaved on one historical period, on five markets — nothing more.
