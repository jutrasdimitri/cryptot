PRICE // SHIPPING // SIZING // RETURNS

How much do CryptoT shirts and hoodies cost?

T-shirts are $35 USD and hoodies are $69.99 USD. That is the full price — international shipping is free on every order, with no hidden fees.

Is shipping really free?

Yes. Every CryptoT order ships internationally at no extra cost, anywhere in the world. One price, shipped free. See shipping & returns for details.

How long will my order take to arrive?

Production takes 2–5 business days, then shipping time depends on your location. Most orders arrive within 2–3 weeks worldwide.

What sizes are available?

S, M, L, XL and 2XL. Our shirts and hoodies are unisex with a classic fit. When in doubt, take your usual size.

Can I return or exchange my order?

Every piece is printed to order, so returns cover defective items only. If your order arrives with a misprint, the wrong item, or damage in transit, we’ll remake it or refund you in full — your choice. Just contact us with your order number and a photo of the issue. Custom orders are final sale, unless defective.

Can I get a design for another cryptocurrency?

Yes. We do custom orders — any ticker, any color. Email us with your request and we’ll make it happen.

How do I pay?

Secure checkout. We accept cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple Pay, Google Pay, Interac e-Transfer, and crypto (BTC, ETH, ADA, XRP, SOL). Your payment info never touches our servers.

Who is CryptoT?

CryptoT is Montreal-based crypto streetwear, est. 2025. Education first: every design carries the idea behind the protocol — Bitcoin, Ethereum, Cardano, XRP and Solana. Read About Them, then wear the signal.
