FAQ_

How much is shipping?

Free. Every order ships internationally at no extra cost — the price you see is the price you pay, shipping included, no hidden fees.

How long does delivery take?

Every piece is printed to order: production takes 2–5 business days. Delivery after that: about 1 week in the US, 1–2 weeks in Canada and Europe, and up to 3 weeks for the rest of the world.

What sizes are available?

S, M, L, XL and 2XL. Our shirts are unisex with a classic fit. When in doubt, take your usual size.

Can I return or exchange?

Every piece is printed to order, so returns cover defective items only. If your order arrives with a misprint, the wrong item, or damage in transit, we’ll remake it or refund you in full — your choice. Just contact us with your order number and a photo of the issue. Custom orders are final sale, unless defective.

What if I want another crypto?

We do custom orders. Any ticker, any color — just ask and we’ll make it happen.

How do I pay?

Secure checkout. Your payment info never touches our servers.
