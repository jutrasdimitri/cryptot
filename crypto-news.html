Week of September 28–October 4, 2026

October 2, 2026

Weak U.S. jobs report cools the Fed hike debate

The September U.S. jobs report came in far below expectations: employers added just 29,000 jobs against forecasts of roughly 84,000–90,000, the unemployment rate ticked up to 4.2% from 4.1%, and July–August figures were revised down by a combined 60,000. Treasury yields fell on the release and market pricing for an October Fed rate hike dropped sharply — a relief valve for risk assets after weeks of tightening fears, and the macro backdrop behind Bitcoin’s push back toward the high $80k range late in the week. One soft report does not end the hiking cycle, but it changed the odds the market was trading on.

Source: IndexBox (jobs data via the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, reported by CNBC)
October 4, 2026

After the record week, ETF flows split: BTC in, ETH out

One week after their record run, U.S. spot crypto ETFs diverged. For the week of September 28 to October 2, Bitcoin products still posted net inflows of about $82.9M — a steep slowdown from the prior week’s ~$2.39B — while Ethereum products recorded about $118M in net outflows, reversing the ~$690M they had just taken in. Solana products added only about $0.8M, down from $188.1M the week before. The takeaway is not a reversal of institutional interest; it is a cooldown after an exceptional week, with the three Power 5 ETF complexes moving in different directions for the first time in weeks.

Source: CoinDesk (flow data via Farside Investors)
October 2, 2026

SEC clears 3x Bitcoin and Ether futures ETFs for listing

The SEC approved a Cboe BZX rule change allowing the listing of six triple-leveraged Volatility Shares funds, including 3x products tracking Bitcoin and Ether futures, alongside gold, silver, oil and gas. These are futures-based funds that reset their leverage daily — not spot products — and that daily reset cuts both ways: one modelled 3x daily Bitcoin strategy was down 44.4% this year even though Bitcoin itself was down only 3.2%. Listing approval is a market-structure milestone, not an endorsement, and leveraged daily-reset products are built for short-term trading, not holding.

Source: Catenaa (on the SEC’s October 2 approval order)
October 6, 2026 (testnet)

Ethereum’s Glamsterdam reaches the Sepolia testnet

Ethereum’s next major upgrade, Glamsterdam, activates on the Sepolia testnet on Tuesday, October 6 at 13:53 UTC (epoch 353,024), per the Ethereum Foundation. The package combines the Amsterdam execution-layer changes with the Gloas consensus-layer changes, headlined by enshrined proposer-builder separation (EIP-7732) and block-level access lists (EIP-7928), plus gas repricing. Sepolia node operators must update both execution and consensus clients before the fork. This is a public rehearsal, not a mainnet launch: the mainnet date remains unscheduled, with only a Q4 2026 target, and nothing is required from ETH holders.

Source: COINOTAG (schedule per the Ethereum Foundation)
September 30, 2026

Petrobras tests Cardano to track lower-carbon fuel

Brazil’s state-controlled energy giant Petrobras is testing Cardano in two research projects announced by the Cardano Foundation on September 30. The first, CS-SAF, built with PUC-Rio University’s Ledger Labs, turns each sustainable aviation fuel attribute into a standardized token on Cardano, with metadata aligned to the CORSIA aviation emissions scheme, so an environmental benefit can be minted, transferred and retired — and claimed only once, closing the double-counting gap in book-and-claim fuel sales. The second tracks Diesel R, Petrobras’ partly renewable diesel, across production and transport to support Scope 3 emissions reporting. Both projects are research-stage: no volumes and no rollout timeline have been disclosed.

Source: Blockchain Reporter via CryptoNews (announcement by the Cardano Foundation)
September 30, 2026

Evernorth clears its merger vote: ~473M XRP headed to Nasdaq

Armada Acquisition Corp. II shareholders approved the business combination with Evernorth Holdings on September 30, clearing the path for what the companies describe as the largest publicly traded pure-play XRP treasury. The deal and related placements have raised over $1B in commitments; at closing, expected October 7, Evernorth expects to hold roughly 473 million XRP, with trading on Nasdaq under the ticker XRPN expected to begin October 8. Investors include Ripple, SBI Group, Pantera Capital, Kraken and GSR. Separately, XRP Asia launched on October 3 at XRP Seoul 2026, an initiative to grow XRP Ledger adoption across Asia-Pacific with regional banks and payment companies.

Source: PR Newswire (Evernorth / Armada II announcement, October 1)
October 1, 2026

Fiserv goes live: North Dakota’s Roughrider Coin settles on Solana

Fiserv’s digital asset platform entered production on October 1, and its first live use case is a state bank stablecoin: Roughrider Coin, a U.S. dollar-backed token governed by the Bank of North Dakota, the only state-owned bank in the U.S. More than 90 participating North Dakota banks and credit unions can access it through Fiserv’s Commercial Center to move money between institutions, with transactions settling on Solana. VersaBank USA issues the coin and handles minting, burning, custody and reserves, while Fireblocks provides the digital-asset infrastructure. Access is permissioned and bank-to-bank only — there is no public trading — but as a settlement rail, this is a production deployment inside real banking workflows, not a pilot announcement.

Source: Blockchain Reporter via CryptoRank (Fiserv announcement, October 1)

Week of September 21–27, 2026

September 24, 2026

Bitget loses ~$387.5M in hot-wallet breach

On September 24 at 18:31 UTC, Bitget detected unauthorized transfers from parts of its hot and warm wallet infrastructure. The exchange first estimated losses at $351.6M, then revised the figure to ~$387.5M on September 25 after tracing additional Zcash and TRON transactions — a fuller count of the same incident, not a second breach. The attacker compromised a backend system, spoofed transaction data, and tricked Bitget’s own authorization process into signing the transfers — an echo of the February 2025 Bybit theft. Stolen assets span Ethereum, several EVM networks, the XRP Ledger, Zcash and TRON, and include XRP and ETH. Mandiant and SlowMist are assisting the investigation; CEO Gracy Chen said the attack is consistent with DPRK-linked techniques. Withdrawals restart in phases from September 28, and Bitget says its User Protection Fund will cover the loss.

Source: Unchained
September 25, 2026

Bitcoin ETFs post biggest inflow week of 2026: ~$2.39B

U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs just closed their strongest week of the year: ~$2.386B in net inflows from September 21–25, the biggest weekly total since October 2025. Monday alone drew ~$999M — the largest single-day inflow since October 6, 2025, and the ninth-largest since launch. It capped seven straight inflow sessions (September 17–25, ~$3B total) and flipped 2026 year-to-date flows back to positive (~+$934M) after sitting near −$5.8B in mid-July. Bitcoin broke the $82–83k ceiling that had capped every rally since early September and tagged $87,386 — its highest print since January 31.

Source: AMBCrypto (flow data via SoSoValue)
September 27, 2026

Vitalik maps Ethereum to 2030; Glamsterdam testnet goes live October 6

Vitalik Buterin published “The Cryptographic World Computer,” an essay laying out Ethereum’s path to 2030 — and said the Hegota upgrade planned for next year may be the last “normal” upgrade recognizable to early users. More immediate: the Glamsterdam hard fork activates on the Sepolia testnet on October 6, 2026 at 13:53 UTC, with mainnet targeted for Q4 2026. The upgrade brings native proposer-builder separation and block-level access lists — parallel transaction processing with less reliance on external relays. ETH spot ETFs added ~$690M on the week while ETH tested $2,800.

Source: CryptoCompass
September 24, 2026

Cardano’s institutional week: Fireblocks support, x402 payments

On September 24, the Cardano Foundation announced Fireblocks will add full support for Cardano Native Tokens — custody, sending and receiving — expected by March 2027, opening the network to the banks, exchanges and fintechs on the Fireblocks platform. Three days earlier, ADA entered the x402 payments SDK, and the Foundation had joined Mastercard’s Crypto Partner Program on September 15. ADA responded with a ~21–26% weekly rally, breaking back above $0.25 and printing its first daily golden cross of 2026.

Source: CVJ.AI
September 24, 2026

XRP Ledger new accounts jump 323%; Batch V1.1 slips to October

The XRP Ledger recorded 11,432 newly created accounts in a single day — a 323% jump over the 30-day average of ~2,700. On the protocol side, the Batch V1.1 amendment (XLS-56) dipped back below the 80% validator threshold, restarting its two-week activation window: earliest activation is now October 9, with the companion fixBatchV1_2 amendment moving through voting alongside it. U.S. spot XRP ETFs added ~$75M on the week — an eleventh straight week of inflows — and Ripple hardened its pipeline with AI-driven code reviews plus a dedicated security team.

Source: ad-hoc-news
September 25, 2026

Solana ETFs smash records: $188M week; Alpenglow hits testnet

U.S. spot Solana ETFs just had their biggest week since launch: $188.21M in net inflows from September 21–25, with all seven funds positive every session. Bitwise’s staking-enabled BSOL took $128.46M — 68% of the total — and the complex has now posted 13 straight weeks of inflows while stablecoin supply on Solana hit an all-time high of $17.3B. On the tech side, the Alpenglow upgrade (150ms block times, fault tolerance raised from 33% to 40%) reached its second testnet on September 24. SOL broke $121, up from $97 at the start of the month.

Source: CoinCentral

Week of September 14–20, 2026

September 15, 2026

CLARITY Act stalls in the U.S. Senate

On September 15, the U.S. Senate failed to advance the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act: the cloture motion fell 49–50, well short of the 60 votes needed. Every Democrat voted no — joined by four Republicans over ethics guardrails and CFTC staffing — leaving the most ambitious federal crypto market-structure bill effectively dead for 2026.

Source: CoinDesk
September 15, 2026

XRP Ledger Batch amendment (V1.1) hits 80% validator support

Amendment XLS-56 — "Batch V1.1" — crossed the 80% validator approval threshold (29 of 35 validators) on September 15, entering its 14-day activation window. Barring a loss of support, it activates around September 29, 2026. The feature bundles up to 8 transactions into a single atomic operation — built for settlement use cases like delivery-versus-payment.

Source: CryptoTimes (announced by Vet / XRPLF, verifiable on-chain via XRPScan)
September 17, 2026

CFTC sends crypto rulemaking to the White House

Two days after the CLARITY Act failed, the CFTC submitted its crypto market-structure rulemaking package ("Regulation Crypto Asset Transactions and Regulation Crypto Asset Markets", RIN 3038-AF80) to the White House's OIRA for review. The draft isn't public yet, but the move signals the agency intends to write crypto market rules under its existing authority. The same day, it issued no-action relief for "passive" software providers.

Source: Unchained
September 17, 2026

SEC grants 5-year "Innovation Exemption" for onchain trading of tokenized stocks

The SEC issued an order granting a conditional 5-year exemption (through September 17, 2031): qualifying "Tokenized Securities Venues" can trade tokenized U.S. NMS stocks onchain through permissioned automated market makers without registering as an exchange. Liquidity providers get a parallel exemption from the "dealer" definition. Tokens must carry the same rights as the underlying shares — dividends, voting, the works.

Source: U.S. SEC (official)

Week of September 7–13, 2026

September 7, 2026

Liquid Network exploit: ~4,000 BTC drained, ~3,400 returned

Blockstream's Liquid Network, a Bitcoin sidechain, was hit by an exploit that drained roughly 4,000 BTC (~$320M). The attackers — described as white hats — agreed to return about 3,400 BTC (~$270M) once the bug was patched, keeping roughly 600 BTC (~$47M) as a bounty. Bridge withdrawals and L-BTC services stayed paused while the incident was investigated.

Sources: The Block, CoinDesk
September 9, 2026

Germany moves to end tax-free crypto after 1 year of holding

Germany's Federal Ministry of Finance drafted a bill that would end the country's famous tax exemption after 12 months of holding. Gains on crypto acquired after December 31, 2026 would be taxed at a flat 25% (+ solidarity surcharge, ~26.375% effective) — like stocks. Existing holdings keep the old regime. The draft still has to clear cabinet and the Bundestag.

Source: CoinDesk
September 10, 2026

Canada's OSFI clears tokenized bank deposits

Canada's federal banking regulator, the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI), issued official guidance: tokenized deposits are "not legally distinct" from traditional deposits. Federally regulated banks can therefore offer blockchain-based deposit products inside the existing framework — no bespoke regime required. A first among major jurisdictions.

Source: CryptoTimes (via OSFI)
