Week of September 14–20, 2026

September 15, 2026

CLARITY Act stalls in the U.S. Senate

On September 15, the U.S. Senate failed to advance the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act: the cloture motion fell 49–50, well short of the 60 votes needed. Every Democrat voted no — joined by four Republicans over ethics guardrails and CFTC staffing — leaving the most ambitious federal crypto market-structure bill effectively dead for 2026.

Source: CoinDesk
September 15, 2026

XRP Ledger Batch amendment (V1.1) hits 80% validator support

Amendment XLS-56 — "Batch V1.1" — crossed the 80% validator approval threshold (29 of 35 validators) on September 15, entering its 14-day activation window. Barring a loss of support, it activates around September 29, 2026. The feature bundles up to 8 transactions into a single atomic operation — built for settlement use cases like delivery-versus-payment.

Source: CryptoTimes (announced by Vet / XRPLF, verifiable on-chain via XRPScan)
September 17, 2026

CFTC sends crypto rulemaking to the White House

Two days after the CLARITY Act failed, the CFTC submitted its crypto market-structure rulemaking package ("Regulation Crypto Asset Transactions and Regulation Crypto Asset Markets", RIN 3038-AF80) to the White House's OIRA for review. The draft isn't public yet, but the move signals the agency intends to write crypto market rules under its existing authority. The same day, it issued no-action relief for "passive" software providers.

Source: Unchained
September 17, 2026

SEC grants 5-year "Innovation Exemption" for onchain trading of tokenized stocks

The SEC issued an order granting a conditional 5-year exemption (through September 17, 2031): qualifying "Tokenized Securities Venues" can trade tokenized U.S. NMS stocks onchain through permissioned automated market makers without registering as an exchange. Liquidity providers get a parallel exemption from the "dealer" definition. Tokens must carry the same rights as the underlying shares — dividends, voting, the works.

Source: U.S. SEC (official)

Week of September 7–13, 2026

September 7, 2026

Liquid Network exploit: ~4,000 BTC drained, ~3,400 returned

Blockstream's Liquid Network, a Bitcoin sidechain, was hit by an exploit that drained roughly 4,000 BTC (~$320M). The attackers — described as white hats — agreed to return about 3,400 BTC (~$270M) once the bug was patched, keeping roughly 600 BTC (~$47M) as a bounty. Bridge withdrawals and L-BTC services stayed paused while the incident was investigated.

Sources: The Block, CoinDesk
September 9, 2026

Germany moves to end tax-free crypto after 1 year of holding

Germany's Federal Ministry of Finance drafted a bill that would end the country's famous tax exemption after 12 months of holding. Gains on crypto acquired after December 31, 2026 would be taxed at a flat 25% (+ solidarity surcharge, ~26.375% effective) — like stocks. Existing holdings keep the old regime. The draft still has to clear cabinet and the Bundestag.

Source: CoinDesk
September 10, 2026

Canada's OSFI clears tokenized bank deposits

Canada's federal banking regulator, the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI), issued official guidance: tokenized deposits are "not legally distinct" from traditional deposits. Federally regulated banks can therefore offer blockchain-based deposit products inside the existing framework — no bespoke regime required. A first among major jurisdictions.

Source: CryptoTimes (via OSFI)
